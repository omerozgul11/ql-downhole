INSTALL IN THREE STEPS
----------------------
Requirements: Windows 10 (21H2 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit; 8 GB RAM
recommended; about 200 MB of disk space. No internet connection is needed.

1. Open the folder "1 - Install Downhole Studio" and double-click
   DownholeStudio-Setup-{version}.exe. No administrator rights are needed.

2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or "Unknown publisher"
   if the installer is not code-signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Accept the licence agreement, leave "Launch Downhole Studio" selected and
   click Finish.

ACTIVATE (NO INTERNET NEEDED)
When Downhole Studio starts, the activation window shows this computer's code
(five groups of five characters). Click "E-mail to QuakeLogic", or copy the
code and send it to sales@quakelogic.net with your company name and order
number. You receive an activation key for this computer: paste it into the
activation window and click Activate (or click "Open Activation File..." and
choose the .dhslic file from the e-mail). The key works only on that computer.

Later, start Downhole Studio from Start > Downhole Studio or the desktop icon.
Installing over an earlier version keeps all your projects and settings.


YOUR FIRST SURVEY
-----------------
1. Read the Field Guide (folder 2) before going to the field. It covers the
   borehole, the P and S sources, the seismograph settings and recording.
2. Watch the Training Video (folder 3) to see the whole analysis once.
3. In Downhole Studio, click "Explore Sample Project" to open a finished
   survey, or create a new project and import the example field records
   (folder 6). The orange button at the top of each page is always the next
   step, and Help (F1) explains every page.
